Identity is found through action, not a quiz.

Kids don’t pick a label. They try things, and the platform watches what they keep coming back to.

  1. Intake

    A short assessment of interests, work style, goals, and current screen habits. It starts a profile. It doesn’t fix an identity.

  2. Creator challenges

    Hands-on, offline challenges: build a LEGO structure from your own blueprint, record a podcast, plan a fundraiser.

  3. Proof through performance

    Completion, time invested, enjoyment, reflections, and portfolio work show what a kid really enjoys.

  4. Creator Identity

    The student gets a primary identity, a strengths profile, career pathway ideas, and a growing portfolio. It keeps evolving.

We find out why a kid reaches for the screen.

KINDFORGE identifies each student’s main engagement driver, then responds with offline activities that meet the same need.

Connection seeking

Uses technology for belonging. We offer collaborative challenges and mentoring.

Exploration seeking

Lacks offline challenge. We offer hands-on projects that spark curiosity.

Stress relief

Uses screens to avoid hard feelings. We offer low-pressure creative activities.

High stimulation

Wants fast, intense input. We offer project challenges with frequent milestones.

Habitual use

Screen use is routine. We build new habits with rewarding offline replacements.

Kids’ data is protected first.

KINDFORGE serves children as young as 8, so privacy is built in from the start.

  • Parental consent at signup, and one-tap permanent deletion at any time.
  • Names and ZIP codes never go into AI processing, and the AI makes no diagnoses or clinical recommendations.
  • All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • We never sell student data. It is written into our Terms of Service.
  • Every school partnership runs under a signed FERPA Data Processing Agreement.

Questions parents and counselors ask.

Does KINDFORGE limit or block screen time?

No. Restriction alone leaves the empty hour behind. KINDFORGE gives kids offline challenges worth choosing instead, so screen use drops because something better is competing with it.

Does AI decide my child’s Creator Identity?

The identity comes from KINDFORGE’s own scoring framework, built on the intake, challenge results, and engagement. An AI-assisted engine reads anonymized data to spot a student’s main engagement driver. It never sees names or ZIP codes, and it makes no diagnoses.

What if my child doesn’t fit one identity?

Identity isn’t fixed. It shifts as kids try new things. We’re also planning an Explorer Path for students whose interests don’t point to one identity yet.

What ages is it for?

Ages 8 to 14. We’re starting with ages 12 to 14 in partner schools, then adding ages 8 to 11.

Who can see my child’s data?

Parents consent at signup and can delete everything with one tap, any time. Student data is never sold. School partners sign a FERPA Data Processing Agreement.

What does it cost?

Families pay $9.99 per month. School subscriptions are coming soon, at $3,500 per year with a free pilot first.
